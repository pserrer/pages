Privacy Policy

BRI Tracker · Last updated 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the app BRI Tracker (Android package com.pserrer.bricalculator) handles information.

In short: BRI Tracker keeps your measurements on your phone. There is no account and no server of ours: we never receive your name, your body measurements or anything about how you use the app. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold to anyone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile. Your height, the name you enter (optional), your preferred units and your optional BRI and BMI targets are kept in a database on your phone.
  • Your measurements. Each waist size, optional weight and the date you recorded them are kept in the same database on your phone.
  • What the app works out from them. BRI, BMI and ABSI, trends, summaries and progress towards your targets are calculated on the phone and are not sent anywhere.
  • Reminders, only if you turn them on. The weekly reminder is scheduled on the phone by the operating system. No push service of ours is involved.

Body measurements can be health-related information. Because they never leave your phone through the app, nobody but you and whoever can use your phone can see them. If your phone’s system backup is turned on, Android or iOS may include the app’s data in your device backup, which is handled by your backup provider under their terms.

When the app talks to anyone else

BRI Tracker does not need the internet and sends nothing about you anywhere. The only exception:

  • Links you open. Tapping “Sources” opens the scientific references on PubMed in your browser. That site sees your visit as it would any other.

Permissions

  • Notifications, only if you turn on the weekly reminder, so it can arrive on time.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, photos or any other personal data on your phone.

Deleting your data

You can delete single measurements or all measurements at once under Profile. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. As we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete.

If you email us, we use your message only to reply and to fix the problem, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

The values shown are for information only and do not replace advice from a doctor.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
