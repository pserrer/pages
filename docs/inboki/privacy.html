Privacy Policy

Inboki · Last updated 18 October 2026

Inboki lets you post links and fetches their content directly on your device. We do not collect your information or store it on our servers.

Links you post and content Inboki fetches are stored locally on your device. The app does not send them to us.

Link requests

When Inboki fetches a link, your device connects directly to the linked website. That website may receive information such as your IP address and details included in the request, and may handle it under its own privacy policy.

Storage and deletion

Inboki stores its data on your device, not on our servers. Uninstalling the app removes the data Inboki stored on that device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and change the “Last updated” date.
